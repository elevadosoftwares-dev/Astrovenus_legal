02 How we use it

  • Run your account — sign-in, identity verification, and account security.
  • Provide the service — astrology calculations, matching you with an astrologer, live chat and voice consultations.
  • Handle payments — process wallet recharges and pay out astrologer earnings.
  • Keep you informed — push notifications and SMS about consultation status, wallet activity, or offers you can opt out of.
  • Support & resolve disputes — using your consultation and transaction history if you contact support.
  • Improve the app — aggregated, non-identifying usage analytics.
  • Meet legal obligations — tax and financial record-keeping for astrologer settlements.

03 Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only where it's needed to run the service:

  • Firebase (Google) — sign-in, push notifications, crash reporting and analytics.
  • Razorpay — processes your wallet recharge payment. We never see or store your card, UPI or bank credentials ourselves.
  • Agora — provides the real-time infrastructure your voice calls run over.
  • The other party in a consultation — a user and astrologer see each other's display name and the chat/call needed to conduct the session.
  • Legal authorities — only when required by applicable law.

04 App permissions

Both apps request only the device permissions each feature genuinely needs, and every one is optional except where noted:

  • Location — confirms your birth place, or your current location if you tap "use current location." A manual search always works without it.
  • Microphone — active only for the duration of a live voice consultation you've started.
  • Notifications — consultation and wallet alerts. Can be turned off in your device settings.
Declining a permission may disable the specific feature it supports (e.g. voice calls without microphone access), but never blocks the rest of the app.

05 How long we keep it

We keep your account, consultation and transaction records for as long as your account stays active, and for as long as applicable financial and tax record-keeping rules require after that. In-app notifications are automatically deleted after 60 days. You can ask us to delete your account and associated data at any time — see Contact us — subject to records we're legally obligated to retain.

06 Your choices & rights

  • Edit your profile and birth details any time from within the app.
  • Decline location or microphone access from your device settings — the relevant feature will prompt you again only if you use it.
  • Request a copy of, correction to, or deletion of your personal data by writing to us (below).
  • Unsubscribe from promotional notifications while still receiving essential account/consultation alerts.

07 Children

AstroVenus is intended for users 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and we remove any account we learn belongs to a child.

08 Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) for all data in transit, and access controls that limit who inside our organization can view your data. No system is perfectly secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security — but we treat a breach of your data as seriously as you'd expect.

09 Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. Material changes will be announced in-app before they take effect; the date at the top of this page always reflects the latest version.
