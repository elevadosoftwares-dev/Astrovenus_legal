Delete Your Account

Applies to the AstroVenus user app and the AstroVenus Astrologer app

You can permanently delete your AstroVenus account at any time, right from the app. This page explains how, what's removed, and what's retained.

How to request deletion

1
Open the app and go to Profile / Settings → Delete My Account.
2
Tap "Send OTP to Confirm" — we'll send a one-time code to your registered phone number.
3
Enter the OTP and tap "Permanently Delete My Account." This takes effect immediately — there's no waiting period and no email to send.

What gets deleted

  • Your phone number and email address are permanently removed from your account
  • Your login access is permanently blocked — you cannot sign back into this account, by OTP or otherwise
  • Your phone number is freed up and can register as a brand-new, separate account going forward
  • Every active session and signed-in device is immediately signed out

What's retained, and why

Deleting an account removes your identity and login access, not the historical records tied to it — those stay in our systems, no longer connected to any contact details or login you control:

DataWhy it's kept
Consultation history, chat messages, call recordsOperational/billing records of past sessions
Wallet recharge and payment transaction recordsFinancial and tax record-keeping obligations under applicable law
Reviews you've writtenKept as part of an astrologer's public record, no longer linked to you
Astrologer settlement, payout, and KYC/verification recordsRequired financial and compliance record-keeping
None of this is accessible to you, to the person it's about, or shown as "yours" anywhere in the app once your account is deleted — it simply isn't erased outright, the way financial and consultation records generally aren't for any platform like this one. If you'd like a specific record fully erased beyond what's described here, contact us (below) and we'll evaluate what's possible under applicable law.

Deactivating vs. deleting

The "Deactivate account" option inside the app's Settings is different from deletion described here — deactivating just hides your account and signs you out; logging back in with your phone number reactivates it automatically, and nothing changes. Deletion is a separate, OTP-confirmed action in the same Settings area.

Deletion is permanent. Once confirmed, you cannot sign back into this account, and this cannot be undone by logging back in or contacting support.
